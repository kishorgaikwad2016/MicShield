Frequently Asked Questions

How is MicShield different from clicking Mute inside Zoom or Teams?

App-level mute buttons only silence that specific app—other background tabs, Electron apps, or voice recorders can still listen to your microphone. MicShield mutes your audio input at the macOS CoreAudio hardware abstraction layer, so zero audio signal reaches any app on your Mac.

On MacBooks with a camera cutout, MicShield hugs the physical notch. On non-notch Macs or external displays, MicShield Pro automatically renders a sleek floating "Virtual Island" pill on your screen, while the center Overlay HUD works on every display free forever.
